Two planned qualitative analyses connect outcomes to mechanisms. The selection
analysis traces feasibility, primary objective, and canonical ordering through
the decoder and downstream report. The numeric analysis traces a permitted input
to a problematic intermediate comparison, then examines whether the model
specified a sufficient guard or fallback. Each analysis should show the initial
claim, evidence returned, revision, and reserved-test scope, including unsuccessful
repairs. Examples are explanatory and do not enlarge the independent sample size.
